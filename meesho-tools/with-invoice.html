📖 How to use this tool

Step 1

Upload one or more Meesho shipping label PDFs using the file picker or drag & drop.

Step 2

Click Start crop — the tool will extract each page as a separate label.

Step 3

Labels are automatically arranged in a 2 × 2 grid on A4 pages.

Step 4

Click Download PDF to save your formatted A4 sheets.

💡 Tip: You can upload multiple PDFs at once — all pages will be combined into one output.
