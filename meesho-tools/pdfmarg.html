📖 How to use PDF Merger

Step 1

Drag & drop PDF files into the upload area or click to browse.

Step 2

Reorder files using the ↑ and ↓ buttons if needed.

Step 3

Click Merge PDFs — the tool combines them in order.

Step 4

Download your merged PDF instantly. All processing is local and private.

💡 Tip: You can upload multiple PDFs at once and reorder them before merging.
