About the Brand

Trendy Handbags & Assamese Heritage

Saheb Trend House was founded by Saheb Ali with a passion for fashion and tradition. We now focus on stylish, durable ladies handbags — perfect for college, office, and daily use — alongside our authentic Assamese traditional jewellery.

Our handbag collection features spacious totes in premium canvas and nylon, with multiple pockets and trendy designs. For special occasions, we offer Bihu and wedding jewellery like Golpata bangles and Junbiri necklace sets, crafted with care.
