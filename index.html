About Saheb Ali

A student entrepreneur driving business expansion and educational initiatives from Western Assam.

I am Saheb Ali, an Economics (Honours) scholar at Gauhati University (Abhayapuri College) and founder of Saheb Trend House. My focus lies at the intersection of marketplace economics, modern supply chains, and digital business systems.

From establishing pan-India e-commerce channels on Meesho, Flipkart, and Amazon to engineering automated label utilities via SAHEB Digital Hub, I build practical, technology-driven solutions for retail and digital commerce.

Additionally, as the founder of EDU SMARTSPARK (eduspark.site), I develop academic guidance resources to support university students across Assam.
